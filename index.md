Short daily cyber security briefings with an Australian focus, published each weekday morning (Perth time), and
periodic Intel reports on the patterns across them.

Each brief summarises the day's notable incidents, advisories and research in our own words and links to the original sources.

## Archive

- [Daily briefs](briefings/daily/): one per weekday, from July 2026.
- [Intel reports](intel/): monthly, quarterly and yearly pattern analysis, with PDFs.
