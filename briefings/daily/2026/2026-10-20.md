# Daily brief · Tue 20 Oct 2026

## Australia

**AI agents used to break into company websites, including Australian ecommerce sites.** A researcher at NorForge
watched a Chinese-speaking attacker hand almost all of a hacking spree to autonomous AI agents, which probed and broke
into websites of more than 100 companies. iTnews reports ecommerce sites in Australia, Canada, the US and Japan were
among those targeted. Review exposed admin interfaces and web application logs for automated probing.

## Vulnerabilities and advisories

**PAN-OS CVE-2026-0257 under active exploitation.** Unit 42 says ransomware operators, including the groups behind
Qilin and Inc, are attempting to exploit an authentication bypass in GlobalProtect portal and gateway components of
vulnerable PAN-OS versions. Patch affected firewalls and check GlobalProtect logs.
Sources: [Unit 42](https://unit42.paloaltonetworks.com/active-exploitation-of-pan-os-cve-2026-0257/)

**Siemens Software Center flaw exploited in ClickFix attacks.** Siemens says attackers have used CVE-2026-110936, a
DLL search-path weakness, in a malware campaign. Version 4.0.9.8 fixes it and installed copies update automatically
on next launch. Sources: [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/siemens-software-center-vulnerability-exploited-in-clickfix-malware-attacks/),
[The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/clickfix-attackers-exploit-siemens.html)

**Critical NGINX and Drupal fixes.** F5 released out-of-band patches for NGINX, including three critical flaws
(CVE-2026-15176, CVE-2026-97205, CVE-2026-93479) that can allow code execution on some non-default configurations.
Drupal patched two critical remote code execution bugs (CVE-2026-108099, CVE-2026-108097) that become exploitable
with common contributed modules, in at least one case without authentication.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/f5-releases-out-of-band-patches-for-critical-nginx-vulnerabilities/),
[SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/drupal-warns-of-fast-exploitation-risk-for-critical-core-rce-flaw/)

## Incidents and threats

**ConnectWise switches off Cloud Backup after Microsoft 365 tenants were accessed.** A stolen ConnectWise application
credential let attackers sign in to customers' Microsoft 365 environments; Huntress confirmed unauthorised sign-ins in
162 tenants. ConnectWise has disabled seven application identities and says it found no evidence stored backups were
accessed. MSPs and their customers should review Microsoft 365 sign-ins and app consents.
Sources: [iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/stolen-connectwise-skykick-credential-exposed-microsoft-365-tenants-629721?utm_source=feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=iTnews+Security+feed),
[The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/connectwise-suspends-cloud-backup-after.html)

**Software supply chains hit three ways.** Google and ManageEngine attribute the hijacking of AOMEI Backupper's update
and notification systems (April to July 2026) to a group tracked as UNC7017. Five OVHcloud CLI releases on GitHub were
swapped for a secret-stealing build for up to 94 minutes on 19 October. Rank Math PRO downloads and updates between
15 and 16 October carried a self-restoring WordPress backdoor; affected sites should be treated as compromised.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-hijack-aomei-update-servers-to-secretly-spread-malware/),
[The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/hackers-hijack-five-ovhcloud-cli.html),
[The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/popular-wordpress-seo-plugin-rank-math.html)

**APT28 linked to breaches of NATO defence suppliers.** Symantec attributes a campaign against two NATO member states,
including a naval shipbuilder and a military aircraft engine maker, to APT28, using a backdoor and a new in-memory loader.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/russian-apt28-breaches-nato-naval.html),
[The Record](https://therecord.media/european-defense-contractors-fancy-bear-espionage)

**Ahold Delhaize restoring services after cyberattack.** The retailer says last week's attack disrupted websites, apps
and store services in Europe and the US. Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ahold-delhaize-cyberattack-affects-services-across-us-and-europe/)

**OpenCode console breach may have exposed AI provider keys.** OpenCode says an intruder reached a legacy system on
16 October; affected Console users were emailed to revoke and replace their OpenAI, Anthropic and Google keys.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/opencode-console-breach-may-have.html)
