# Daily brief · Fri 23 Oct 2026

## Australia
**Campbelltown Anglican Schools Council investigating a cyber incident.** The Gentlemen ransomware group has listed St Peter's Anglican School, run by the council in Sydney's west, on its leak site. The council confirmed an unauthorised party accessed part of its systems, has engaged forensic specialists and notified the ACSC and OAIC. It says it will contact affected people if personal information is involved.
Sources: [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14340-exclusive-campbelltown-anglican-schools-council-investigating-cyber-incident-following-ransomware-claims)

**Sydney design firm Virtual Ideas hit by Lamashtu ransomware.** The Surry Hills visualisation studio confirmed it is responding to an attack after the Lamashtu group listed it and claimed to have published company data. Virtual Ideas says it has informed staff, clients and the ACSC. Cyber Daily reports this is Lamashtu's first Australian victim.
Sources: [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14337-exclusive-sydney-based-visualisation-firm-virtual-ideas-hit-by-lamashtu-ransomware-attack)

## Vulnerabilities and advisories
**PHP2Shell now broadly exploited; first attempts came four days before patches.** Amazon Threat Intelligence says it saw attempts against CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 from 17 October, while the flaws were still under embargo, and multiple actors firing exploits within hours of disclosure. It now describes exploitation as broad. Anyone who hasn't installed PHP 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 or 8.2.35 should do so now and check internet-facing PHP servers for compromise back to 17 October.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/amazon-says-php2shell-exploitation-began-4-days-before-patches/), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/someone-tried-to-exploit-php2shell-4.html), [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/php2shell-exploitation-started-days-before-patches/)

**Arista VeloCloud Orchestrator zero-day exploited.** CVE-2026-114625 (CVSS 9.9) lets any logged-in user, even a read-only customer account, gain superuser operator rights on on-premises VeloCloud Orchestrator. Arista says it is being exploited. Install the fixed VCO release for your release train, restrict the web interface to admin networks and review recent admin activity. Earlier fixes for the July and September flaws don't cover it.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/arista-patches-new-velocloud-orchestrator-zero-day-exploited-in-attacks/), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/arista-warns-of-critical-velocloud-sql.html)

**eMudhra quits the TLS certificate business.** After eight of its intermediate CA keys were found to be trivially recoverable, the Indian certificate authority says it is withdrawing from global SSL/TLS certificates. Revocation services will continue while customers move to other providers. Organisations using emSign certificates should plan replacements.
Sources: [The Record](https://therecord.media/indian-tech-company-announces-withdrawal-certificate-business-incident), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/emudhra-to-exit-global-ssltls-business.html)

## Incidents and threats
**Microsoft ties ConnectWise attack to Chinese state actor using AI agents.** Microsoft says Storm-0940, a China state-aligned group, used the ARTEX AI penetration testing framework to abuse ConnectWise Cloud Backup application identities from 15 October, after a large password-spray campaign. The group has targeted hundreds of organisations since September, sometimes reaching privileged cloud access in under three hours. Review OAuth app credentials and consents in Microsoft 365 tenants.
Sources: [The Record](https://therecord.media/china-cyber-connectwise-ai), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/china-linked-hackers-use-artex-ai.html), [Infosecurity](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/microsoft-chinese-ai-connectwise/)

**Railway customer secrets may have been accessed.** The hosting platform says an intruder reached systems storing customer environment variables and in some cases obtained decrypted values. It has rotated its own credentials and invalidated API tokens. Railway users should rotate every secret and registry credential stored there and review service logs.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/railway-says-intruder-may-have-accessed.html), [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/railway-warns-devs-to-rotate-secrets-after-potential-breach/)

**Capcom breach exposes 11.7 million account email addresses.** Capcom says attackers accessed its CAPCOM ID membership service, exposing email addresses and login metadata. Passwords and card details were not exposed. Expect Capcom-themed phishing.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/capcom-confirms-data-breach-affecting-117-million-accounts/)

## Law enforcement and policy
**Interpol's Serengeti III: 1,462 arrests across Africa.** Police in 20 African countries and the US disrupted more than 10,000 malicious networks tied to fraud, ransomware and business email compromise, and recovered more than US$105 million. Interpol says nearly 66,000 victims were identified.
Sources: [The Record](https://therecord.media/africa-cybercrime-interpol-arrests), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/interpol-arrests-1462-suspects-disrupts.html)
