# Daily brief · Mon 26 Oct 2026

## Australia
**ABC wipes staff devices exposed to spyware-linked ads.** The ABC says ads from a network tied to Israeli ad-tech firm Discover-Tech were shown on a small number of its devices. It found no sign of compromise but is wiping them as a precaution. Citizen Lab says the network profiles targets through ordinary online ads for spyware vendor Candiru. It also found a large cluster of ad impressions in Australia, including on government, energy, university and nuclear research networks.
Sources: [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14347-aunty-hack-abc-wipes-staff-devices-after-discovering-spyware-campaign)

**ASD's ACSC critical alert on Fortinet products.** ASD's ACSC issued a critical alert on 23 October as Fortinet released critical security updates across several products. The ACSC says it is not aware of active exploitation. Organisations running Fortinet gear should check Fortinet's advisories and apply the updates.
Sources: [ASD's ACSC](https://www.cyber.gov.au/alert/critical-vulnerabilities-in-fortinet-products)

**Australia sanctions North Korean IT worker networks.** The government imposed financial sanctions and travel bans on two North Korean individuals and two companies that place IT workers in remote jobs abroad. The Australian Sanctions Office warns these workers use stolen or doctored Australian licences and passports to win contracts. Breaching the sanctions carries up to 10 years' jail. Check the identity of remote contractors carefully.
Sources: [iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/australia-sanctions-north-korean-it-worker-bosses-and-financiers-629860?utm_source=feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=iTnews+Security+feed)

## Vulnerabilities and advisories
**PHP2Shell attacks escalate, and PHP flags a separate unpatched issue.** Unit 42 reports an intrusion at a European government entity where attackers, apparently using AI agents, reached password hashes and MFA secrets within two hours of exploiting CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619. Mirai-style botnet operators are also trying the flaws, though other firms have not confirmed successful compromises. Separately, PHP warned of CVE-2026-115560, a code execution risk in an unusual session configuration, and advises setting session.upload_progress.enabled=0 until the next release.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/php2shell-exploitation-hits-government.html), [Unit 42](https://unit42.paloaltonetworks.com/cve-2026-109618-cve-2026-109619/), [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/mirai-botnet-operators-join-php2shell-attacks-as-aslr-bypass-exploits-emerge/), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/urgent-php-warns-of-another-unpatched.html)

**Collabora Online flaw exploited to backdoor Nextcloud servers.** CISA added CVE-2026-110447 (CVSS 9.2), a command injection flaw in Collabora Online's built-in CODE server for Nextcloud, to its KEV list, along with the Arista VeloCloud flaw reported on Friday. Attackers are using it to plant web shells and hunt for credentials and crypto wallets. Update the CODE server app to the September fixed release.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/cisa-flags-two-actively-exploited.html)

**HPE fixes eight maximum-severity ArubaOS flaws.** HPE patched 72 vulnerabilities in AOS-8 and AOS-10, including eight unauthenticated code execution and authentication bypass bugs. Patch Aruba controllers, gateways and access points and restrict management interfaces to a dedicated network segment.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hpe-patches-eight-max-severity-rce-and-auth-bypass-aos-flaws/)

**Brotli "Node2Shell" bug reaches far beyond Node.js.** CVE-2026-111497 (CVSS 9.2), an out-of-bounds write in Google's Brotli decompression library, can allow code execution. Researchers have shown an exploit against Node.js, and the flaw also affects Bun, Python packages such as Pillow, Go, .NET, browsers and Electron apps. Watch for vendor updates and patch Node.js now.
Sources: [iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/node2shell-flaw-embedded-in-apps-and-dev-tools-far-beyond-nodejs-629874?utm_source=feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=iTnews+Security+feed), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/brotli-decoder-flaw-behind-nodejs-rce.html), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/researchers-demonstrate-node2shell-rce.html)

## Incidents and threats
**Edge-device campaign exposed more than 70,000 credentials.** Group-IB and NyxLab tie a long-running campaign against exposed FortiGate, FortiWeb and GlobalProtect devices to an access broker known as k0t, who they say has links to the Qilin and The Gentlemen ransomware groups. About 38% of victims are in Asia-Pacific. Patch edge devices, enforce MFA on VPNs and look for unexpected remote access tools.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/hackers-breach-enterprise-firewalls-and.html)

**Quest Diagnostics confirms patient records exposed.** The US lab company says an attacker accessed its Quanum Lab Services Manager system, which clinicians use to order tests and view results. It learned of the access on 21 October.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/quest-diagnostics-confirms-data-breach-exposed-patient-records/), [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/quest-diagnostics-reports-cyber-breach-affecting-certain-patient-records/)
