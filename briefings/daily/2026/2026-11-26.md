# Daily brief · Thu 26 Nov 2026

## Australia
**Bendigo Bank to pay $8 million over controls behind a 2023 account breach.** The Federal Court accepted APRA's case that the bank breached its executive accountability obligations through weak customer authentication controls at its former Alliance Bank, where attackers made 286 unauthorised transactions from about 257 accounts in 2023. The case shows regulators holding executives to account for authentication controls.
Sources: [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14492-bendigo-bank-to-pay-8m-penalty-for-breaching-obligations-following-2023-cyber-attack)

**O'Brien Group Australia finds "no evidence" of breach after LockBit listing.** The hospitality group says it has seen no data posted and no ransom contact since LockBit 5.0 listed it on 24 November, and that its systems are running normally; it has informed ASD's ACSC. LockBit says it will publish within 14 days.
Sources: [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14493-exclusive-o-brien-group-australia-finds-no-evidence-of-breach-following-lockbit-claims)

**Parliament passes the Digital Duty of Care Act.** The law requires large online platforms to assess and reduce foreseeable harms, with protections for under-18s, annual risk assessments and penalties that could reach billions of dollars.
Sources: [The Record](https://therecord.media/australia-passes-online-safety-legislation-preempt-harms)

## Vulnerabilities and advisories
**New critical flaw in Danfoss supermarket controllers, and older ones now exploited.** CISA warns of CVE-2026-129713 (CVSS 9.8), an unpatched flaw in Danfoss AK-SM 800A controllers that allows unauthenticated crashes and possibly code execution, and has added three older Danfoss flaws to its exploited list. These controllers were used in the European supermarket attacks. Keep them off the internet and reach them only through a VPN.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisa-warns-of-critical-unpatched-flaw-in-danfoss-refrigeration-controllers/), [CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/11/25/cisa-adds-10-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)

**CISA flags five exploited Mitel MiCollab flaws, plus Juniper and Xen Orchestra.** The same update lists five exploited MiCollab flaws, a Juniper Session Smart command injection (CVE-2026-103406) and the Xen Orchestra flaw (CVE-2026-133469). Patch MiCollab and keep it off the open internet.
Sources: [CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/11/25/cisa-adds-10-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)

**Palo Alto fixes the kernel bug attackers chained with PHP2Shell to root firewalls.** PAN-OS updates fix Fragnesia (CVE-2026-46300), which attackers used after PHP2Shell to get root on firewalls with internet-facing management. Apply the update and keep management interfaces off the internet.
Sources: [Infosecurity](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/palo-alto-fragnesia-php2shell-fix/)

**Hex.pm package registry servers hacked through a decade-old flaw.** An attacker ran commands on six Hex.pm servers; the team patched within 15 minutes and found no altered packages, but can't rule out access to account data. Elixir and Erlang developers should change their hex.pm password, reset two-factor authentication and log back in.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/hackers-exploited-cvss-100-hexpm-rce.html)

**Two GeoServer flaws exploited within days of disclosure.** watchTowr sees attacks on CVE-2026-120705 and CVE-2026-129243, which allow unauthenticated file reads and protected operations. Upgrade GeoServer to 3.0.2, 2.28.6 or 2.27.7.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/hackers-exploit-two-geoserver-flaws-as.html)

## Incidents and threats
**Microsoft: Salt Typhoon and other state groups now use the ARTEX AI framework.** Microsoft says Chinese state groups including Salt Typhoon, plus North Korean actors, use ARTEX for reconnaissance, credential attacks and web exploitation. Separately, ARTEX was reportedly used against unclassified CIA, ODNI and NGA systems.
Sources: [The Record](https://therecord.media/microsoft-china-salt-typhoon-using-artex-ai-tool), [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/11/25/what-defenders-need-to-know-artex-agentic-ai-framework-threat-actors-abuse/), [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-reportedly-breached-cia-intelligence-analysis-system/)

**US Treasury's FinCEN takes systems offline after an intrusion.** The financial intelligence agency says an intruder reached a limited subset of its systems and has extended some filing deadlines.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/us-treasurys-fincen-takes-some-systems.html), [Infosecurity](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/us-treasury-cyber-incident/)

**Suspected ransomware halts cancer treatment at Guadeloupe's main hospital.** Surgeries and radiation therapy were suspended; France's ANSSI is examining whether attackers came in through a compromised IT supplier.
Sources: [The Record](https://therecord.media/guadeloupe-ransomware-hospital-cyber)
