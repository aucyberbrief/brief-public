# Daily brief · Fri 27 Nov 2026

## Australia
**Flight Centre investigates a breach of a third-party accommodation booking system.** Flight Centre Travel Group told the ASX that unusual activity in a provider's system used by Flight Centre, Travel Associates, Cruiseabout and some independent agents may have exposed a significant number of customer records. Most hold names and basic travel details; a very small share include passport details, contact details, dates of birth or, rarely, health or mobility information. It has found no access to payment or bank details, and flight and corporate bookings are unaffected. Customers should watch for travel-themed phishing that quotes real bookings.
Sources: [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14501-hacked-flight-centre-travel-group-discloses-data-breach-significant-number-of-customers-impacted), [iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/flight-centre-probes-data-breach-of-third-party-booking-system-630641?utm_source=feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=iTnews+Security+feed)

**ANZ warns of job scams as Christmas hiring ramps up.** The National Anti-Scam Centre says job scam reports doubled in 2025 and losses rose 81.5% to $24.8 million. Jobseekers should verify recruiters through the employer's own website and never pay or hand over bank details to get a job.
Sources: [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14498-tis-the-season-anz-warns-of-job-scams-as-christmas-hiring-ramps-up)

## Vulnerabilities and advisories
**Drupal: the installer fix left a gap, and attackers are using it.** CVE-2026-136716 lets attackers take over sites left in unusual or incomplete install states even after last week's fix, and Drupal says it's being exploited. Update to 11.4.14, 11.3.23 or 10.6.23, run update.php, lock down install.php, and check logs and the user 1 account for unexpected changes.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/drupal-warns-of-new-highly-critical.html)

## Incidents and threats
**1Password breached through an employee's GitHub account.** An attacker used a hijacked employee GitHub account to push malicious code to 1Password's backend services, taking customer names, email addresses, account IDs and recovery and invitation tokens, plus internal source code. 1Password says vault contents and encryption keys were not accessed, and the stolen tokens can't be used without SSO or an emailed code. Customers should expect convincing phishing using their 1Password details.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/1password-customer-data-stolen-after-employee-github-account-breach/), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/1password-hacked-customer-data-and.html), [Infosecurity](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/1password-github-account-backend/)

**North Korea-linked PolinRider has planted malware in thousands of GitHub repositories.** SafeDep found a Python loader in test and utility files that pulls its next stage from Ethereum transactions, part of a campaign that also uses fake recruitment take-home tests. Review any repository you clone from strangers, especially conftest.py and small utility packages.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/north-korea-linked-polinrider-poisons.html)

**Fake .NET job tests deliver malware through NuGet packages.** Socket found 13 malicious NuGet packages, posing as WinUI and WPF button controls, used in a fake take-home assessment; building the project downloads and runs malware. Developers should do job tests in a disposable virtual machine.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/13-malicious-nuget-packages-turn-fake.html)

**SYS010 malware enrols browsers in fake enterprise management to read Gmail and Outlook.** Bitdefender says Meta ads for free animated wallpapers install malware that puts Chrome under attacker-controlled management and force-installs an extension that reads and deletes email, targeting Facebook business accounts. Check for unexpected "managed by your organisation" notices in Chrome.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/sys010-malware-abuses-browser-management-to-spy-on-gmail-outlook/)

**Phishers use Hugging Face's AI site builder to spoof Microsoft, PayPal and Netflix.** Aikido found 42 phishing pages hosted on Hugging Face Spaces, most built with its DeepSite AI tool. Don't trust a login page because it sits on a reputable domain.
Sources: [Infosecurity](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/hugging-face-ai-builder-phishing/)

**North Korean launderers move Binance proceeds through THORChain and coinjoin.** Researchers say they are using the exchange the FBI asked not to help them, and a mixing service; about US$2.85 billion of the US$6.3 billion stolen has been frozen.
Sources: [iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/north-korean-launderers-turn-to-little-known-crypto-services-630651?utm_source=feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=iTnews+Security+feed)
