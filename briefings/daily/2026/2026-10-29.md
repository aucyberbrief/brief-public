# Daily brief · Thu 29 Oct 2026

## Australia
**RMS Cloud incident leads to phishing of Australian holiday park guests.** Booking platform RMS Cloud, used by more than 6,000 holiday parks, hotels and campgrounds, confirmed a global incident. Guests are getting email, SMS and WhatsApp messages that quote genuine booking details and ask for card payments. Reflections Holidays, BIG4 and G'day Parks have warned customers, and RMS is reissuing credentials to integration partners. Accommodation providers on RMS should warn their guests, and anyone who paid through one of these messages should contact their bank.
Sources: [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14364-aussie-holiday-park-operators-compromised-by-rms-cloud-third-party-breach)

## Vulnerabilities and advisories
**China-nexus attackers chain PHP2Shell with a Linux kernel bug to root firewalls.** Security firm Dream says a suspected China-nexus actor combined PHP2Shell with Fragnesia (CVE-2026-46300) to root 25 firewalls from one vendor in the US, Japan, Taiwan and South Korea. On two firewalls at a US government-affiliated organisation, the attacker went on to attack Active Directory. CISA has added Fragnesia and a VMware ESX logging flaw (CVE-2026-41709) to its KEV list. Patch the PHP and kernel components in network appliances as vendors release fixes.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/php2shell-attack-roots-25-servers.html), [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/china-linked-hackers-exploit-php2shell-to-root-firewalls/), [CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/10/28/cisa-adds-two-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)

**Node2Shell Brotli flaw now exploited.** CISA and Sophos report active exploitation of CVE-2026-111497 in the Brotli decoder, and honeypots are logging attack attempts. US federal agencies must patch by 10 November. Update Node.js, .NET, Python Brotli bindings and any other software that decompresses untrusted Brotli data.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisa-warns-of-hackers-now-exploiting-critical-node2shell-flaw/), [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/cisa-warns-of-node2shell-exploitation-as-honeypots-log-attack-attempts/), [Sophos](https://www.sophos.com/en-us/blog/node2shell-cve-2026-111497-in-active-exploitation)

**Attackers probing new Oracle E-Business Suite RCE flaw.** Defused's honeypots logged exploit attempts against CVE-2026-101767, an unauthenticated flaw that leads to remote code execution, a week after Oracle's October patch. Oracle hasn't marked it as exploited. Apply the October Critical Patch Update to internet-facing EBS now.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-now-scanning-systems-for-recently-patched-oracle-flaw/), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/hackers-target-critical-oracle-e.html), [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/hackers-start-probing-fresh-oracle-ebs-vulnerability/)

**Tailscale fixes the Tailworm SSH flaws.** Tailscale 1.104.4 fixes the socket-forwarding flaws that let an attacker on a trusted device reach root-only sockets on other servers through Tailscale SSH. Update Linux and macOS nodes that run Tailscale SSH.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/tailscale-patches-ssh-flaws-that-could.html)

## Incidents and threats
**Volt Typhoon used a covert "Silent Train" network for zero-day attacks.** Lumen's Black Lotus Labs says the Chinese state group builds disposable chains of compromised devices to reach high-value edge and management systems. It has used this network to exploit at least four zero-days, in Versa Director, N-able N-central, Arista VeloCloud Orchestrator and Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN. Targets include telcos, government, defence, utilities and MSPs, and Lumen says the focus appears to be the Indo-Pacific.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/volt-typhoon-hides-zero-day-attacks-using-silent-train-framework/), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/volt-typhoon-used-covert-silent-train.html)

**GMO urges customers to disconnect after hacker's ransomware threat.** The attacker who breached Japan's Onamae Desktop Cloud threatened a ransomware attack, and GMO has confirmed company and customer data were published. GMO told customers to shut down and disconnect PCs, reset MetaTrader trading passwords and end active sessions. It is offering a US$1 million bounty for information on the attackers.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/japans-gmo-urges-customers-to-shut-down-pcs-amid-ransomware-threat/), [Infosecurity](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/japan-gmo-internet-disconnect/), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/japans-gmo-offers-1-million-bounty-to.html)
