# Daily brief · Fri 30 Oct 2026

## Australia
**Australian law firms among the world's most targeted.** CrowdStrike's 2026 Legal Threat Landscape Report puts Australia among the eight countries where law firms faced the most malicious activity in the year to August. Australian legal practices ranked third globally for initial access broker listings, behind the US and Brazil, with access selling for about US$1,000 on average. CrowdStrike says financially motivated groups such as the Silent Ransom Group (Chatty Spider) drive most of it, sometimes posing as IT staff. Law firms should harden remote access and verify help-desk contacts.
Sources: [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14370-report-aussie-law-firms-increasingly-targeted-by-sophisticated-hackers)

## Vulnerabilities and advisories
**Cisco firewall VPN zero-day exploited to crash devices.** Cisco says attackers have been exploiting CVE-2026-76418 in ASA and Firepower Threat Defense software this month, using crafted SSL VPN requests to force reloads when secondary authentication is configured. CISA has added it to KEV. Upgrade to a fixed release, or disable secondary authentication on affected tunnel groups until you can.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisco-warns-of-firewall-zero-day-exploited-to-crash-ssl-vpn-devices/), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/cisco-patches-actively-exploited-asa.html), [CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/10/29/cisa-adds-two-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)

**PHP2Shell exploited against Palo Alto management interfaces.** Palo Alto Networks says attackers are exploiting the PHP2Shell flaws (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619) against management interfaces exposed to the internet. GreyNoise reports at least six successful exploitations. CISA warns the flaws are being chained with the Fragnesia Linux bug (CVE-2026-46300) to get root, and US agencies must patch by 31 October. Take firewall management interfaces off the internet and apply vendor fixes.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/attackers-exploit-php2shell-flaws-in.html), [The Record](https://therecord.media/php2shell-successful-exploitation-reports-emerge), [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisa-warns-of-fragnesia-linux-flaw-chained-in-php2shell-attacks/), [Unit 42](https://unit42.paloaltonetworks.com/cve-2026-109618-cve-2026-109619/)

**GitLab CVSS 10 flaw added to KEV; self-built servers also need Ruby updates.** CISA added GitLab's CVE-2026-97229 to KEV. Alpine Linux says an attacker tried to exploit it on Alpine's own GitLab server, a week after the fix shipped. Alpine's image used standard Ruby, so upgrading GitLab alone didn't close the hole. Alpine rebuilt the server and reset every password. If you run GitLab on your own Ruby (from source or your own container), update Ruby as well as GitLab.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/attacker-tried-to-exploit-alpine-linuxs.html), [CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/10/29/cisa-adds-two-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)

**Linux kernel TCP flaw gets an AI-written root exploit.** Anthropic says its model wrote an exploit for CVE-2026-98432, a use-after-free in the kernel's TCP connection setup, that gives a remote attacker root on servers with a multipath TCP listener. The kernel team rates it 9.8, though distributions score it lower. Debian, SUSE, Oracle and Amazon Linux have fixed kernels; Ubuntu and RHEL did not as of 29 October. Patch where fixes exist.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/linux-kernel-tcp-flaw-with-98-cvss.html)

## Incidents and threats
**KeyDrift malware stole SD-WAN encryption keys through a Cisco flaw.** Cisco Talos says a group it tracks as UAT-11473 exploited CVE-2026-20127, the Catalyst SD-WAN authentication bypass fixed in February, to plant malware that copies the keys protecting traffic between sites. Targets included space, defence and policy research organisations. Talos says the group's infrastructure overlaps heavily with Volt Typhoon's Silent Train network. Check SD-WAN controllers patched late for compromise and rotate keys.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-exploit-cisco-sd-wan-flaw-to-deploy-custom-keydrift-malware/), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/hackers-exploited-cisco-sd-wan-flaw-to.html)

**China-nexus Warp Panda used a VMware ESX zero-day to hide from audit logs.** CrowdStrike says Warp Panda exploited CVE-2026-41709 as a zero-day in early 2026 to perform admin actions without logging, in attacks on the US legal sector. CISA has added it to KEV. Apply Broadcom's July fixes and forward ESX logs off-host.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/china-linked-hackers-exploited-vmware.html)
