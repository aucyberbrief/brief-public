# Daily brief · Mon 23 Nov 2026

## Australia
**Bell Financial says client data was likely accessed.** The Australian stockbroker and financial services firm says an intruder reached a limited number of its IT systems and that some clients' data may have been accessed; the incident is contained and client assets are safe. It has notified ASD's ACSC and the AFP and is working with the OAIC. Clients should expect phishing or calls that use their account details and confirm any contact through Bell's known numbers.
Sources: [iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/australias-bell-financial-says-some-client-data-likely-accessed-630479?utm_source=feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=iTnews+Security+feed)

**Hitachi Vantara tells staff, including Australians, that a 2025 breach exposed bank details.** Current and former employees of Hitachi Vantara and related Hitachi companies are being told that their bank account numbers, employee IDs and salary information were taken in an April 2025 incident, which the company described at the time as ransomware. Affected staff should watch for payroll-redirection and bank-impersonation scams.
Sources: [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14469-exclusive-hitachi-vantara-informs-employees-including-aussies-of-data-breach)

**JPMorgan, Visa and Mastercard confirm vendor breaches after n0n leaks.** The extortion group n0n posted data it said came from the three companies; each says a third-party provider was breached and that customer names and email addresses, not payment data, were exposed. Visa says its affected provider is in the Asia-Pacific region. Cardholders should expect phishing that names their card program.
Sources: [iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/jpmorgan-visa-mastercard-say-third-party-vendors-targeted-in-cyberattack-630480?utm_source=feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=iTnews+Security+feed), [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14470-chase-bank-visa-and-mastercard-confirm-third-party-cyber-attacks), [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/vendor-data-breach-exposes-chase-visa-mastercard-customer-info/)

## Vulnerabilities and advisories
**Microsoft says an exploited Entra Connect Health flaw exposed tenant data.** A flaw in the Entra Connect Health API, introduced on 23 July and found on 9 November, let attackers read tenant-level metadata, including administrator email addresses and configuration details, for affected organisations. Microsoft says no configuration changes were made and has notified affected tenants. Warn admins named in tenant records about targeted phishing.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/microsoft-entra-connect-health-flaw-exploited-to-steal-tenant-data/)

**GL.iNet cloud flaw used to plant malware on routers and KVM devices.** Attackers exploited GL.iNet's GoodCloud remote management service to install backdoors and reverse shells on customers' routers and remote-access KVM devices. The cloud flaw is fixed, but infected devices stay infected. Check devices, install the new firmware released on 22 November, and turn off cloud management if you don't need it; KVM devices give direct control of the computers attached to them.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-exploit-glinet-cloud-flaw-to-infect-routers-kvm-devices/)

**CISA adds vCenter, Cisco Expressway, Apache Roller and SAP NetWeaver flaws to its exploited list.** All four are now confirmed exploited. Attackers used the Cisco Expressway flaw (CVE-2026-76466) to run root commands on four devices and reach a Russian organisation's internal network soon after exploit code appeared. If you haven't patched vCenter (CVE-2026-123890) since Friday's brief, do it now.
Sources: [CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/11/20/cisa-adds-four-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/hackers-exploit-critical-cisco.html)

**F5 issues emergency fixes for five critical BIG-IP flaws.** Stack overflows in BIG-IP Access Policy Manager and iRules can let unauthenticated attackers crash the traffic engine or run code in vulnerable configurations. Patch BIG-IP APM deployments promptly.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/f5-releases-emergency-patches-for-critical-big-ip-rce-flaws/)

**SonicWall: Gen 6 firewalls will get no more fixes, even for critical flaws.** SonicWall expects internet-facing Gen 6 devices to be actively targeted with AI-assisted scanning and urges customers to replace them with Gen 8 now and take them off the internet edge meanwhile.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/sonicwall-warns-gen-6-firewalls-wont.html)

**Ghostscript "Upload2Shell" turns image uploads into server compromise.** CVE-2026-125396 lets PostScript disguised as a PDF or image run commands on servers that process uploads with Ghostscript before 10.08.0; researchers confirmed it in 10 products including Debian 13 and Roundcube. No attacks are reported, but proof-of-concept details are public. Update Ghostscript, including copies bundled inside applications.
Sources: [iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/ai-found-ghostscript-flaw-turned-file-uploads-into-remote-code-execution-630512?utm_source=feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=iTnews+Security+feed), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/researchers-demonstrate-ghostscript.html)

**Unpatched Windows "FastUnlock" lets someone holding a locked PC run programs.** The researcher behind SignDrop published a lock-screen bypass that uses Narrator and account recovery options to run programs in a limited account; it needs physical access and has no fix yet. Keep BitLocker on and don't leave laptops unattended.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/new-unpatched-windows-0-day-fastunlock.html)

## Incidents and threats
**China-linked attackers' AI agents found and exploited a medical-imaging zero-day.** Amazon says the attackers told the Cairn AI framework to "obtain shell access" and its agents found a path-traversal flaw in MONAI Label, open-source medical-imaging labelling software, gaining root on a university server. MONAI Label 0.8.5 and earlier are affected and run with no login by default; a fix is merged but not yet released. Don't expose MONAI Label to untrusted networks and turn on authentication.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-use-autonomous-ai-tool-cairn-to-find-zero-day-take-over-server/), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/attackers-ai-agents-found-zero-day-in.html), [The Record](https://therecord.media/amazon-research-cairn-ai-university-attacks-possible-zero-day)

**Bell Canada says an "agentic" attacker stole enterprise customer data.** An intruder reached a pre-production copy of the system Bell uses to order and provision network circuits on 17 November, taking business contacts, ticket details and some network IP information.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/bell-canada-says-agentic-hacker-stole-enterprise-customer-data/)

**Rackspace Spot breach exposed customers' Kubernetes secrets.** Attackers used leaked credentials on 17 November to act as users in some customer organisations, create pods and read Kubernetes secrets. Rackspace Spot customers should rotate every secret stored in their clusters.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/rackspace-spot-breach-let-hackers-access-customer-kubernetes-secrets/)

**Cleverbridge breach exposes card numbers and CVVs.** The e-commerce platform behind software sales for vendors including Red Hat, SUSE, Tenable and Veeam says attackers copied stored payment card data after an intrusion detected on 4 November. Anyone who bought software through a Cleverbridge checkout should watch their card or request a replacement.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cleverbridge-warns-hackers-stole-credit-card-numbers-cvvs-in-data-breach/)

**FBI reportedly breached again, this time its national crime database.** CNN reports the FBI is investigating an intrusion into the National Crime Information Center used by police across the US, two months after an earlier breach claim. The FBI hasn't confirmed it.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/fbi-reportedly-hacked-again-this-time-targeting-nationwide-police-database/)
