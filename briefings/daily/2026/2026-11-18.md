# Daily brief · Wed 18 Nov 2026

## Australia
**ASD's ACSC issues an "Act Now" alert on the pace of vulnerability exploitation.** The ACSC says publicly reported vulnerabilities rose 30% last financial year and some compromises now happen within 48 hours of a patch being published, helped by automated and AI-enabled tools. It asks organisations to patch faster and turn on automatic updates where suitable, make sure suppliers can patch the systems they run for you, cut unnecessary internet exposure, strengthen MFA and access controls, and deal with legacy systems. Where you can't patch at once, restrict exposure and add temporary controls.
Sources: [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14455-act-now-aussie-cyber-agency-warns-of-growing-pace-of-vulnerability-disclosures)

**Stolen Australian cards sell for about $22 on criminal forums.** NordVPN says Australian cards fetch about 45% more than US ones, and talk of loading stolen cards into Apple Pay and Google Pay has nearly tripled in a year, with criminals using phishing and SIM swaps to beat wallet verification. Banks should scrutinise new wallet provisioning, and customers should never share one-time codes.
Sources: [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14453-report-just-22-can-score-you-a-stolen-aussie-bank-card-on-underground-forums)

## Vulnerabilities and advisories
**Apache Axis flaw exploited against Confluence Data Center and N-central.** Rapid7 saw CVE-2026-90436 (CVSS 9.8) exploited against Confluence Data Center on 13 November; the same flaw underpins the N-central exploit. Confluence 9.x and earlier are vulnerable when the Remote API is on: upgrade to 9.2.28 or 10.x, or turn off the Remote API. N-able's Hotfix 3 fixes 17 more N-central flaws and removes the legacy APIs entirely; on-premises customers should install it now.
Sources: [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14450-patch-now-hackers-targeting-critical-vulnerability-impacting-atlassian-n-able-products), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/n-able-patches-17-n-central-flaws-as.html)

**Oracle fixes 11 remotely exploitable Java flaws as "Java4Shell" claim emerges.** Oracle scheduled a Java SE update for 17 November fixing 11 flaws that can be exploited remotely without authentication. Separately, Calif says it found a critical OpenJDK flaw (CVE-2026-114938) affecting 17.0.6 and later, whose details leaked; it isn't yet confirmed whether Oracle's update covers it. Apply Oracle's Java update and watch OpenJDK vendors for fixes.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/oracle-to-patch-11-java-flaws-today-as.html)

**Puppet Enterprise flaw exploited, CISA says.** CISA has added CVE-2026-115731, a deserialisation flaw in Perforce Puppet Enterprise, to its exploited list. Puppet controls server configuration at scale, so patch and check for unexpected changes.
Sources: [CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/11/17/cisa-adds-one-known-exploited-vulnerability-catalog)

## Incidents and threats
**Optimizely flaw used to plant skimmers on CNN, BBC, Zoom and other sites.** An attacker exploited a flaw in Optimizely Web Experimentation to inject code into scripts loaded by at least 178 organisations' websites. The code stole card details and swapped crypto wallet addresses, and on iPhones tried to load an exploit kit. Optimizely says it has fixed the flaw and removed the code, but one site was re-infected after cleaning. Sites using Optimizely should review their snippets and project change history, and lock down who can edit them.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/hijacked-optimizely-scripts-served-card.html), [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/optimizely-supply-chain-attack-delivers-malware-to-cnn-fox-news-bbc-salesforce-sites/), [The Record](https://therecord.media/optimizely-malicious-code-customer-sites), [Sophos](https://www.sophos.com/en-us/blog/hijacked-optimizely-snippets-served-malicious-code)

**Japan takes the Fugaku supercomputer offline after a breach.** RIKEN says an intruder got in through Fugaku's public status dashboard between 10 and 13 November, planted malware and took user details, SSH private keys, access keys and research files from users' systems. Researchers who used Fugaku or Rikyu should revoke and replace any credentials stored there.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/japan-takes-fugaku-supercomputer-offlline-after-cyberattack/)

**Infoblox staff data stolen through a data-migration vendor.** The security company says employees' and dependants' bank, birth-date and Social Security details were taken through a compromised third-party vendor; it says its products and build systems weren't involved.
Sources: [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14454-hacked-infoblox-discloses-third-party-data-breach-employee-data-compromised)

**Uber: service provider breach exposed 150,000 driver licences.** Uber says stolen credentials from a service provider were used to access a cloud environment it had set up for that provider, exposing licence images and details of 149,346 people in the US. Review the data and access you give providers, and delete it when the work ends.
Sources: [The Record](https://therecord.media/uber-says-150000-impacted-third-party-breach), [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/uber-notifies-drivers-of-data-breach-affecting-nearly-150-000-people/)

**Google: Chinese state hackers are building their own version of ARTEX.** Google's AI threat report says Chinese state-backed and criminal groups are heavy users of AI attack frameworks, and that government groups are developing their own ARTEX-like tool for espionage.
Sources: [The Record](https://therecord.media/chinese-state-group-developing-automated-attack-tool-espionage)

## Law enforcement and policy
**Alleged INC and Lynx ransomware affiliate extradited to the US.** A Kazakh man arrested in Italy in 2025 faces charges over attacks on US hospitals and schools by the "8-Digits Team", which prosecutors say made more than US$500 million in ransom demands.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/alleged-inc-lynx-ransomware-affiliate-extradited-to-us-faces-27-years-in-prison/), [The Record](https://therecord.media/kazakh-suspected-extradited-charges-8-digits-team-inc-lynx)

**Google Project Zero will cut its disclosure deadline to 30 days.** Citing AI-accelerated exploitation, Project Zero dropped its 30-day grace period on 13 November and will move to 60 days in February 2027 and 30 days in January 2028. Vendors and patch teams should plan for faster disclosure.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/google-project-zero-cuts-bug-disclosure-deadline-to-30-days-amid-ai-threats/)
