# Daily brief · Thu 12 Nov 2026

## Australia
**78% of Australian ransomware victims now pay, McGrathNicol survey finds.** The consultancy surveyed 1,000 Australian business decision-makers. Successful breaches fell to 30% from 37% last year, but 78% of breached organisations paid, up from 64%, four in ten paid within 24 hours and 41% skipped negotiation entirely. McGrathNicol says plans and insurance aren't enough without realistic testing. Rehearse the ransom decision, including who decides and on what evidence, before an incident forces it.
Sources: [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14433-ransomware-report-78-per-cent-of-australian-victims-pay-up-as-businesses-abandon-negotiations)

## Vulnerabilities and advisories
**N-able ships N-central fixes; attackers are already probing.** N-central 2026.4 HF2 fixes the most critical of the disclosed flaws, including XXE, OGNL injection, path-traversal code execution and root privilege escalation (CVE-2026-125878 to CVE-2026-125883). All hosted servers have been upgraded. Defused has seen probing of the exploit paths, including unauthenticated agent sessions and configuration pulls. On-premises customers should follow N-able's upgrade guidance before bringing servers back online, then review admin accounts and agent activity.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/n-able-releases-security-hotfixes-for-zero-day-vulnerabilities/), [Infosecurity](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/n-able-n-central-able2shell-0day/), [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14432-n-able-takes-cloud-service-offline-says-customers-should-follow-suit-following-mass-vulnerability-disclosure)

**Fortinet publishes its November fixes, including critical FortiOS flaws.** Fortinet released nearly 100 fixes, about 17 of them critical, across FortiOS, FortiManager, FortiAnalyzer and other products, after warning that full details would follow on Patch Tuesday. No exploitation was reported at release. Given how fast recent Fortinet and PHP flaws were weaponised, update internet-facing FortiGate, FortiManager and FortiMail devices this week.
Sources: [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/fortinet-patches-nearly-100-vulnerabilities/), [Fortinet PSIRT](https://www.fortiguard.com/psirt)

**Critical Zoom Workplace flaw on Windows.** CVE-2026-108186 (CVSS 9.6) and CVE-2026-108187 allow unauthenticated privilege escalation over the network in Zoom Workplace for Windows. No exploitation is reported. Update to 7.1.8 or later.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/critical-zoom-workplace-flaw-could.html)

**eMudhra revokes eight more certificate authorities after its key mishap.** The CA says the same in-house script behind October's weakened keys also pulled the keys of eight RSA intermediate CAs out of their hardware security modules, and it can't show they were handled properly. It revoked them, six for TLS and two for code signing. Check for certificates chaining to eMudhra intermediates and replace them.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/emudhra-revokes-8-more-cas-it.html), [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/emudhra-revokes-8-more-ca-certificates-after-private-key-mishap/)

## Incidents and threats
**Maersk confirms a breach at an acquired logistics business as WhiteArmor demands US$5 million.** Maersk says a local system belonging to a company it acquired was breached and data taken, with no impact on operations or connected customer systems. WhiteArmor claims about 100GB from its contract-logistics platform. Partners exchanging data with Maersk's contract-logistics arm should watch for phishing that uses shipment details.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/maersk-confirms-data-breach-as-whitearmor-ransomware-demands-5-million/)

**AntiFox AI pentest agents used to steal AWS production secrets.** Arctic Wolf says attackers running AntiFox, a fork of the ARTEX framework, from Alibaba Cloud exploited web services, planted web shells and used stolen IAM credentials and EC2 roles to make hundreds of AWS API calls in minutes, in some cases reaching production secrets. Alert on bursts of API calls from unfamiliar cloud addresses and scope IAM credentials tightly.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-use-antifox-ai-agents-in-aws-credential-theft-attacks/), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/antifox-ai-pentesting-platform-linked.html)

**Railway: stolen customer AI keys ran up about US$740,000.** The platform says October's attacker decrypted environment variables in up to 1.85% of workspaces and hunted for AI provider keys; Railway will cover charges that providers won't refund. Rotate any secrets stored on Railway and set spending limits on AI provider keys.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/railway-says-stolen-customer-ai-keys.html), [iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/app-hoster-railway-attackers-ran-up-us740000-on-stolen-ai-keys-630277?utm_source=feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=iTnews+Security+feed), [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/apple/railway-breach-leads-to-740-000-in-fraudulent-ai-charges/)

**Poland takes its ePUAP government services platform offline after an attack.** The digital affairs minister says the attack on the platform citizens and businesses use to deal with government was detected and stopped, and the shutdown was precautionary.
Sources: [The Record](https://therecord.media/poland-shuts-down-government-services-platform-cyberattack)

## Research

Find out which AI app builders staff use and what they've published.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/over-35000-grok-built-apps-exposed.html)

**Check Point links three China-nexus campaigns to one malware supplier.** It ties clusters in Latin America, Asia and Europe to a shared Sparrow framework and a suspected supplier it calls CopperDragon; one used compromised sites with fake Chrome certificate warnings to push a "certificate fix".
Sources: [Infosecurity](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/chinese-malware-tied-three-hacking/)

## Law enforcement and policy
**Pall Mall Process adopts first guidelines for commercial hacking firms.** Fourteen governments led by the UK and France endorsed non-binding guidelines asking spyware and intrusion-tool vendors to vet customers, build in safeguards against misuse and report abuse.
Sources: [The Record](https://therecord.media/britain-france-set-out-guidelines-for-commercial-hacking-firms)
