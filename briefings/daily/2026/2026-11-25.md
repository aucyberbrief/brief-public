# Daily brief · Wed 25 Nov 2026

## Australia
**HYFLOCK publishes data it says came from Darwin accountancy TCA, including tax file numbers.** Cyber Daily has seen documents from the leak, mostly dated 2010–2018 with some from 2026, including business activity statements, tax file numbers, BSBs, bank account numbers and ABNs. The group first listed TCA on 10 November and claims 60GB. TCA hasn't commented. Clients should watch for ATO or bank impersonation scams, and accounting firms should purge old client records they no longer need.
Sources: [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14487-exclusive-darwin-based-accountancy-firm-listed-by-hyflock-ransomware)

**ASX-listed Peninsula Energy halts trading after a cyber incident at its US uranium mine.** The Australian company says the incident hit its Lance project in Wyoming and it is still working out the timing and impact.
Sources: [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/peninsula-energy-halts-trading-after-cyber-incident-hits-us-uranium-mine/)

**NSW sets up a joint police and Cyber Security NSW command centre.** The pilot embeds Cyber Security NSW staff with NSW Police cybercrime teams to share intelligence and coordinate response to incidents affecting government agencies and residents.
Sources: [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14490-nsw-government-launches-joint-agency-cyber-command-centre), [iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/nsw-police-and-cyber-agency-set-up-joint-command-centre-630588?utm_source=feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=iTnews+Security+feed)

## Vulnerabilities and advisories
**Silverstripe fixes critical flaws that let anyone take over CMS sites.** One flaw (SS-2026-008, CVSS 9.8) lets an unauthenticated form submission, including on the login page, run code on the server in a stock install; another lets anyone sign in to accounts with no password, including the default admin. Silverstripe is widely used by government agencies and banks. Patch now; if you can't, turn off allow_url_fopen, restrict admin paths and audit passwordless accounts.
Sources: [iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/silverstripe-patches-critical-flaws-that-let-attackers-take-over-cms-sites-630580?utm_source=feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=iTnews+Security+feed)

**Another critical vCenter authentication bypass, days after the last zero-day.** Broadcom fixed CVE-2026-123903 (CVSS 9.8) in the same vCenter directory service, plus a file-write flaw that can lead to code execution, among 23 VMware fixes. Apply the new builds, such as vCenter 8.0 U3m and Cloud Foundation 9.1.2, even if you patched last week.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/vmware-vcenter-hit-by-another-critical.html)

**CISA confirms the Drupal, Optimizely and Idunno.AntiCsrf flaws are exploited.** The Drupal installer flaw (CVE-2026-132165) from last week is now being exploited. If you haven't upgraded Drupal to 10.6.22, 11.3.22 or 11.4.13, do it now.
Sources: [CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/11/24/cisa-adds-three-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)

**Check Point management servers breached at 22 organisations.** GreyNoise says an actor it calls GT13 compromised Check Point Security Management servers at government, finance, energy and other organisations in 17 countries, exploiting a SmartConsole authentication bypass (CVE-2026-16232) after getting in. It believes the campaign is still active. Patch management servers, restrict SmartConsole access and check for unfamiliar admin sessions.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-compromise-check-point-servers-in-22-organizations/)

**WHMCS flaw exposes hosting customers' data without login.** CVE-2026-82981, a SQL injection in the WHMCS shopping cart used by many web hosts, can expose client records and admin password hashes. Self-hosted operators should upgrade to 9.0.10, 8.13.8 or 9.1.2.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/whmcs-sql-injection-flaw-could-expose.html)

## Incidents and threats
**New "Inception" extortion brand runs large SSO vishing campaign.** Okta links Inception to a ShinyHunters-associated group that phones staff to get SSO passwords and MFA approvals; its leak site names firms including AMD and PepsiCo, none of which have confirmed a breach. Remind staff that IT won't call asking them to approve MFA or log in through a link.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/okta-links-shinyhunters-associated.html), [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/new-inception-extortion-gang-launches-large-scale-vishing-campaign/), [Infosecurity](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/inception-extortion-sso-vishing/)

**Radware breach exposes customers' cloud log-export keys.** An internal Cloud WAF management API reachable from the internet without authentication let an attacker read customer contacts and, for some, credentials for AWS S3, Azure Blob or SFTP log exports. Affected Radware customers should rotate those credentials.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/radware-discloses-breach-exposing-customer-cloud-access-keys/)

**Turnitin investigates a breach of customer-facing systems.** The plagiarism-checking service, used by many universities, says systems remain safe to use and it has notified regulators.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/turnitin-discloses-cyberattack-affecting-customer-facing-systems/)
