# Daily brief · Mon 2 Nov 2026

## Australia
**Only 4% of Australian organisations regularly test their response to AI incidents.** ISACA's State of Cybersecurity 2026 research finds few Australian organisations rehearse how they would handle an AI-related incident, such as staff leaking data into a generative AI tool or an AI-enabled fraud attempt. Globally, almost half of respondents don't know whether their organisation has an AI incident playbook, and nearly a third have run no AI-related exercises. Add an AI scenario to your next incident response exercise and make sure responsibilities are clear.
Sources: [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14374-op-ed-just-4-of-australian-organisations-regularly-test-their-response-to-ai-cyber-incidents)

## Vulnerabilities and advisories
**Ivanti Connect Secure exploit chain gives attackers unauthenticated root.** CISA added two Ivanti Connect Secure flaws (CVE-2026-111357 and CVE-2026-118527) to its KEV list after Volexity found a group it calls UTA0576 chaining them with an authentication bypass (CVE-2026-104935) to get root on the VPN appliance. The attackers used that access to pull credentials, Active Directory details and BitLocker recovery keys rather than plant malware. Fixes for all three flaws are now out. Upgrade Connect Secure now. If your appliance was exposed this month, treat stored credentials and BitLocker keys as compromised and rotate them.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/attackers-chain-three-ivanti-connect.html)

**Drupal Commerce flaw exploited against online stores.** Drupal warns that a "highly critical" missing-authorisation flaw in Commerce Core (CVE-2026-118539) is being exploited, and says it can hit sellers financially. Upgrade to Commerce Core 3.3.13 or 8.x-2.41, and check recent orders for anything you didn't expect.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/drupal-warns-of-critical-commerce-core-flaw-exploited-in-attacks/), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/hackers-actively-exploit-highly.html)

**WordPress core flaw drew tens of thousands of attack attempts within a day.** A race condition in WordPress core updates (versions 4.7 to 7.1.3) can let an unauthenticated attacker run code while a full update is unpacking. Wordfence logged nearly 63,000 attempts in 24 hours after the 28 October fix, with no confirmed compromises so far. Make sure sites have taken the patched release, and block web access to the wp-content/upgrade folder if you can.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/wordpress-rce-flaw-draws-nearly-63000.html)

**Critical LXD and MicroCloud flaw lets attackers impersonate trusted clients.** Canonical's CVE-2026-112816 (CVSS 10) lets anyone who can reach the LXD or MicroCloud HTTPS API and holds a trusted client's public certificate log in as that client, up to root on the host if the client is privileged. Update to the fixed releases, and keep these APIs off untrusted networks.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/critical-lxd-and-microcloud-flaw-cvss.html)

**TeamViewer fix stops a compromised host attacking the technician who connects to it.** CVE-2026-111585 (CVSS 8.8) lets a malicious machine being supported crash, and on some platforms run code on, the viewer's computer once a session starts. Update the TeamViewer Full Client, Portable and Android Remote Control apps, and the Host, to 15.83; help-desk and MSP staff are the obvious targets.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/teamviewer-flaw-lets-remote-computers.html)

**Android "SMS of Death" exploit published.** A proof of concept for CVE-2026-58882 lets a crafted text message crash unpatched Android phones or cut their mobile service, including during emergency calls, with no user interaction. Push the latest Android security update to managed devices.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/new-android-sms-of-death-exploit-can-remotely-crash-unpatched-phones/)

## Incidents and threats
**FBI blames North Korea's TraderTraitor for the US$6.4 billion Binance theft.** Binance says attackers used a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop to reach a service container and push unauthorised withdrawals, the largest crypto theft on record. The FBI attributes it to TraderTraitor (Jade Sleet), which typically tricks targets into running malware through fake recruiter approaches, and says the stolen funds are being converted mainly into Ether for laundering. About US$2.85 billion has been frozen. Crypto and fintech firms should treat developer and DevOps workstations as high-value targets and be wary of job-themed lures.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/fbi-links-north-koreas-tradertraitor-to.html), [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/fbi-links-63-billion-binance-crypto-heist-to-north-korean-hackers/), [iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/binance-freezes-assets-after-us63-billion-heist-630029?utm_source=feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=iTnews+Security+feed), [Infosecurity](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/binance-record-hack-crypto-heist/)

**Trojanised Claude Code extension on Cursor's marketplace planted SSH backdoors.** A malicious version 2.1.313 of the Claude Code extension, published under its real extension ID, was served through Cursor's marketplace for about 11 hours on 29 October. Cursor says at most 5,000 users were affected. It added an attacker's SSH key, labelled "svc-telemetry", to authorized_keys, tried to open a reverse SSH tunnel and set up persistence. Copies from the official VS Code Marketplace and Open VSX weren't affected. Cursor users who updated that day should remove the key, check scheduled tasks, cron and launch agents, and rotate credentials on those machines.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/official-claude-code-vs-code-extension.html), [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/malicious-claude-code-extension-on-cursor-planted-ssh-backdoors/), [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/10/30/inside-malicious-claude-code-branded-cursor-extension-persistent-ssh-backdoor/), [Sophos](https://www.sophos.com/en-us/blog/malicious-claude-code-extension-distributed-through-cursor-marketplace)

**ARTEX AI framework spreads to a second Chinese state group.** CrowdStrike says the group it tracks as Vapor Panda, which overlaps with Earth Estries, used the open-source ARTEX framework against more than 200 organisations from 17 October. Targets included foreign ministries, defence, semiconductor and telecommunications organisations. Google's threat intelligence team describes a wider shift to agentic offensive frameworks that let small teams attack many targets at once. Expect faster, broader scanning and credential attacks.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/chinese-hackers-use-ai-powered-artex-to.html), [iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/artex-ai-hacking-framework-spreads-to-another-china-linked-group-630045?utm_source=feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=iTnews+Security+feed), [Google Threat Intelligence](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/artex-and-friends-enter-agentic-offensive-frameworks/)

**Another developer platform breach: Northflank tells customers to rotate everything.** After an alert from Wiz, the cloud hosting platform Northflank said customers' AI API keys were being abused and told them to assume all environment variables were decrypted and stolen. Railway disclosed a similar breach last week. Northflank users should rotate every key and secret stored there.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/northflank-security-breach-leads-to-abuse-of-customers-ai-api-keys/)

**Chainalysis breached by an attacker using "offensive agentic techniques".** The blockchain analysis firm says an outsider got into its systems and internal tools and accessed customer names, emails, user IDs and role labels, but not investigation data or credentials.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/chainalysis-discloses-data-breach-from-offensive-agentic-techniques/), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/chainalysis-confirms-intrusion-into.html)

**Hijacked third-party script drained about US$2.7 million from Polymarket traders.** Attackers took over DebugBear's DNS through its domain registrar and swapped the page-speed script Polymarket loaded, which then moved signed-in traders' positions to the attackers. Polymarket is refunding 726 wallets. Review third-party scripts on sensitive pages, and use subresource integrity or self-hosting where you can.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/hackers-steal-about-27-million-from.html)

**Trezor warns of a suspected hardware implant in a wallet sold by a reseller.** A customer reported an extra circuit board with a mobile modem inside a Trezor Safe 5 bought from CryptoBilis. According to the report, the board reads the recovery phrase off the screen and sends it out; Trezor has yet to examine the device. Trezor tells anyone who bought from that reseller since March to move funds to a new wallet with a new recovery phrase. Buy hardware wallets only from the maker or an authorised reseller.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/trezor-warns-of-suspected-hardware.html), [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/trezor-warns-of-suspected-crypto-theft-implant-in-hardware-wallet/)
