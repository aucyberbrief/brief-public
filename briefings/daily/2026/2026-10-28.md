# Daily brief · Wed 28 Oct 2026

## Australia
**ASD cut off an Amadey botnet from 180,000 infected computers.** ASD says its operators got into the backend of a criminal's Amadey malware operation and disconnected the controller from about 180,000 victims worldwide, including some in Australia. In a separate operation, ASD destroyed 5 terabytes of stolen business data, including an estimated 4.8 million records about Australians, before it could be sold. Cutting off a botnet doesn't undo past theft, so organisations that found Amadey or StealC infections should still reset credentials and revoke sessions.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/australia-gets-into-amadey-malware.html)

**ASD threat report: 82,000 cybercrime reports and an 80% jump in notifications.** In 2025-26, ASD's ACSC received more than 82,000 cybercrime reports (about one every six minutes), responded to more than 1,500 incidents and warned more than 4,400 organisations of possible malicious activity, 80% more than the year before. ASD calls AI "the defining force" in the threat landscape. Average reported losses were $33,000 for individuals and $93,500 for businesses. ASD urges organisations to fix vulnerabilities quickly and plan for service disruption.
Sources: [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14354-report-aussie-cyber-agency-received-more-than-82-000-cybercrime-reports-in-12-months)

**Qilin lists four Australian victims in four days.** Cyber Daily reports that Qilin listed Melbourne's Creative Universe, CDG Chartered Accounting, Race Trakka (deregistered in 2023) and Bathurst RSL between 22 and 26 October. Bathurst RSL has confirmed an incident; the others did not respond. Cyber Daily says Qilin has listed 52 Australian organisations since 2022.
Sources: [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14358-exclusive-qilin-ransomware-group-claims-quartet-of-aussie-victims-in-as-many-days)

## Vulnerabilities and advisories
**CISA confirms PHP2Shell exploitation and adds Brotli and Magento flaws to KEV.** CISA now says CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 are being exploited. It also added the Brotli decoder flaw (CVE-2026-111497) and the Magento PolyShell file upload flaw (CVE-2026-48356) to its KEV list. Finish patching PHP, Brotli-dependent runtimes such as .NET and Node.js, and Adobe Commerce and Magento.
Sources: [CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/10/27/cisa-adds-two-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog), [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisa-confirms-php2shell-vulnerabilities-now-exploited-in-attacks/), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/cisa-confirms-active-exploitation-of.html), [iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/fixes-for-google-brotli-flaw-arrive-for-microsoft-net-php-and-linux-629928?utm_source=feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=iTnews+Security+feed)

**Tailscale rushing fixes for "Tailworm" flaws published before patches.** Researchers released details of 11 flaws in Tailscale and the self-hosted headscale server before fixes were ready. The worst lets an authorised user on a tailnet get root through Tailscale SSH. Until patches land, consider disabling Tailscale SSH and tighten tailnet access rules.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/tailscale-warns-of-serious-ssh-flaws-allowing-root-privilege-escalation/), [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/tailscale-rushes-patches-after-uncoordinated-disclosure-of-tailworm-flaws/)

**Apple backports exploited CoreGraphics fix to older iPhones and iPads.** iOS and iPadOS 18.7.11 now fix CVE-2026-86950, which Apple says may have been exploited against targeted people. iOS 27.2 and the other new releases fix hundreds more bugs. Update managed Apple devices, including older models.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/apple-backports-fix-for-exploited.html), [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/apple/apple-backports-coregraphics-zero-day-patches-to-older-iphones/)

## Incidents and threats
**University of New Mexico Health diverts ambulances after cyberattack.** The state's only Level 1 trauma centre diverted ambulances for about two hours and cancelled outpatient surgeries on Monday while it contained an attack. It has resumed appointments, and law enforcement is investigating.
Sources: [The Record](https://therecord.media/university-of-new-mexico-hospital-diverts-cyber-incident), [Infosecurity](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/cyber-unmhealth-surgeries/), [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/cybersecurity-issue-forces-a-major-new-mexico-hospital-to-divert-patients-and-close-clinics/)

**Teams external chats used to breach organisations in under 15 minutes.** Arctic Wolf saw more than 400 incidents where attackers email-bombed staff, then posed as IT support in external Teams chats to get remote access and run payloads. Restrict external Teams access to trusted domains and train staff to verify help-desk contact.
Sources: [Infosecurity](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/exploit-teams-chat-breach/)

**Shared analytics script hijacked to skim cards from Brazilian stores.** Sansec says a skimming group modified a self-hosted analytics tracker run by e-commerce platform Convertr, which loads on every merchant's checkout. Sansec suspects the group exploited an outdated Plausible install (CVE-2026-8467). Merchants should review third-party scripts on checkout pages.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-hijack-convertr-analytics-script-to-steal-payment-card-data/)

## Law enforcement and policy
**Nefilim and Hive ransomware affiliate jailed for 70 months.** Ukrainian national Artem Stryzhak was sentenced in the US for ransomware attacks with Nefilim and Hive, including attacks on organisations in Australia in 2021. His alleged co-conspirator Volodymyr Tymoshchuk remains at large.
