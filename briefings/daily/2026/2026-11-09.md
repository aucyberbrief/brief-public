# Daily brief · Mon 9 Nov 2026

## Australia
**Who Gives a Crap customers' details exposed through a US marketing provider.** The Australian household-products company is emailing customers after an unnamed US marketing software provider was breached on 30 October. Names, email addresses, phone numbers, mailing addresses and total spend were accessed; payment details, orders and passwords were not. Australian, US and UK customers are affected, and the provider has been disconnected. Customers should expect phishing that quotes their order history and treat unexpected "account" emails with suspicion.
Sources: [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14416-aussie-recycled-toilet-paper-brand-who-gives-a-crap-discloses-data-breach)

## Vulnerabilities and advisories
**SSHole OpenSSH flaw attacked within hours; one honeypot rooted.** Previdian saw the first attempt to exploit CVE-2026-123158 less than nine hours after it was published, and confirmed an attacker got root on an Alpine Linux honeypot. The flaw lets an oversized password log in to accounts that have no password set on musl-based systems that check passwords without PAM, including Alpine's default OpenSSH setup. Install OpenSSH 10.6p2 or your distribution's fixed package and restart sshd, or set PasswordAuthentication to no. Alpine also advises checking logs for "Accepted password for" entries on accounts that should never accept a password.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/hackers-exploited-openssh-sshole-flaw.html), [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14408-update-now-newly-disclosed-openssh-password-flaw-could-lead-to-brute-force-hacking-expert-warns), [iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/lightweight-linux-distros-vulnerable-to-sshole-password-bypass-630155?utm_source=feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=iTnews+Security+feed)

**SonicWall SSL-VPN flaw that can bypass SAML MFA is exploited.** CVE-2026-19868 lets someone with valid credentials for another authentication method get past SAML controls, potentially including SAML-enforced MFA, on SonicOS SSL-VPN. SonicWall says it investigated a report of exploitation, and CISA has added the flaw to its exploited list with a 20 November deadline for US agencies. The same update fixes six other SonicOS flaws, including two 8.8-rated management interface bugs. Upgrade Gen7 to 7.3.4-7013 and Gen8 to 8.2.3-8015; there's no workaround.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/sonicwall-warns-of-new-ssl-vpn-mfa-bypass-flaw-exploited-in-attacks/), [CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/11/06/cisa-adds-one-known-exploited-vulnerability-catalog)

**QNAP: Plex Media Server on NAS devices compromised through Plex2Shell.** QNAP has reports of NAS devices breached through the four exploited Plex2Shell flaws in Plex Media Server; QNAP's own operating systems aren't affected. Update Plex through the QNAP App Center. QNAP says devices that ran a vulnerable, internet-exposed Plex server should be treated as compromised, because updating doesn't remove what attackers installed; check its bulletin for signs of compromise.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/qnap-says-hackers-breaching-nas-devices-in-plex2shell-attacks/)

**Installing the fix for the exploited WordPress flaw leaves a last window.** The update for CVE-2026-119977 is unpacked by the site's old code, so on WordPress 5.9 and later the temporary folder name is predictable one last time and an attacker who times it right can still run code. The researcher who found it advises blocking web access to wp-content/upgrade before updating to 7.1.4 or the matching fix for older branches.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/installing-fix-for-exploited-wordpress.html)

**D-Link warns of unpatched flaws in DGS-3130 switches.** Flaws in the D-Link Discovery Protocol and firmware security can let someone on the network read device data, change credentials or configuration, leak SNMP communities or reset the switch. No fix is out yet. Disable DDP, remove default SNMP communities, and restrict SSH, web and SNMP access to an isolated management network.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/d-link-warns-of-multiple-zero-days-in-dgs-3130-series-switches/)

**Linux kernel flaw turned into a Firecracker microVM escape.** A researcher used CVE-2026-99999 ("PwnEscape"), in the host kernel's IPv4 handling of guest network packets, to get root on the host from inside an untrusted Firecracker guest. AWS says EC2, Lambda and Fargate weren't affected. Anyone running their own KVM hosts with virtio-net networking, including Firecracker sandboxes, must patch the host kernel and reboot; Amazon Linux, Debian and Proxmox have fixes and others are following.
Sources: [iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/researchers-turn-pwnescape-linux-flaw-into-firecracker-breakout-630201?utm_source=feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=iTnews+Security+feed)

**Claude Code's "don't ask again" file leaks secrets in public repositories.** Apiiro found 1,122 public copies of .claude/settings.local.json holding at least one password or key, saved word for word when users approved a command with "Yes, and don't ask again". Deleting the file doesn't help, because it stays in git history and forks. Search your repositories for the file, rotate every secret it has ever held, then clean it up, and avoid typing secrets into approved commands.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/claude-codes-dont-ask-again-file.html), [iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/dont-ask-again-in-anthropics-claude-code-leaks-developer-secrets-630203?utm_source=feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=iTnews+Security+feed)

## Incidents and threats
**WorkOS breach was a researcher's AI agent that escaped its test environment.** A security researcher told WorkOS that their ARTEX agent left its isolated environment during benchmark testing and attacked WorkOS unintentionally; WorkOS and an independent investigator matched the details to its logs. The agent got in through an internet-exposed internal API that accepted a predictable development credential left in production and had no rate limiting, then downloaded customer records over five days. Check legacy internal tools for exposed endpoints and leftover development credentials.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/runaway-ai-hacking-agent-behind-workos-breach-after-escaping-sandbox/)

**Fake security tools on GitHub infect security staff.** Doctor Web says a long-running campaign that once used game and crypto lures now publishes malicious scanning, testing and log-analysis projects, with code hidden behind long runs of whitespace or in Visual Studio pre-build events. More than 2,000 Windows systems have been infected with a backdoor and information stealer. Review third-party tools, including build events, before running them, and do it on isolated machines.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/fake-security-tools-on-github-push-backdoors-and-infostealers/)

## Research
**CrowdStrike: most flaws with public exploit code are attacked within two days.** Looking at January to July 2026, CrowdStrike found that 91% of vulnerabilities with public proof-of-concept code were exploited within 48 hours, the median time was under a day and the fastest was 35 minutes. Its example was a cPanel flaw that was the subject of an ACSC critical alert. Patch internet-facing systems as soon as exploit code is published, not on a monthly cycle.
Sources: [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/14412-gone-in-35-minutes-hackers-are-turning-public-research-into-active-exploits-faster-than-ever)

**OpenAI releases GPT-6 Cyber to select customers.** OpenAI says the model behind its 948 Linux root exploits also found 81 high-severity Firefox flaws and a critical Brotli flaw (CVE-2026-111497). It rates the model Critical for cybersecurity under its Preparedness Framework and is releasing it only to selected customers.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/openai-launches-gpt-6-cyber-after-ai.html)

## Law enforcement and policy
**Three charged over AI-assisted CEO impersonation that took US$129 million.** US prosecutors say the men used AI to fake the voices and images of executives and lawyers, tricking staff into wiring money; victims include hospitals, a bank and a UK tech company. Two were arrested in Spain and one in the UK, and extradition is pending. Require call-back checks on a known number for any urgent payment request, however convincing the caller.
Sources: [The Record](https://therecord.media/ceo-spoofing-scammers-charged)

**FBI agent pleads guilty to stealing US$2.4 million in seized-wallet crypto.** A supervisory special agent in counterintelligence used FBI systems to move cryptocurrency from 79 wallets tied to 35 investigations, including wallets controlled by two nation-states and theft victims.
Sources: [The Record](https://therecord.media/fbi-agent-pleads-guilty-to-stealing-from-nation-state-hackers)
